Node.js 坑：http(s).request 显式设置 Host 头会带偏 TLS SNI（servername 取 Host 头值）。经反代/CDN 按 SNI 路由的链路（如 tailscale funnel），Host 头与连接目标域名不一致会被直接断连，报 'Client network socket disconnected before secure TLS connection was established'。修法：显式传 servername，或让 Host 头与目标域名一致
